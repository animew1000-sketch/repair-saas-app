Project Notice

Privacy, Terms & California Repair Service Notice

This notice describes the current Motivo prototype, information users enter, and limitations of its repair-service workflows. It is not a substitute for a legally reviewed privacy policy or commercial agreement.

1. Senior Project / Prototype Status

Motivo connects customers with repair shops for demonstration, coursework, testing, and educational use. It is not presented as a production-ready legal, regulatory, accounting, payment-processing, or compliance system.

Before commercial use, a repair business must review privacy, licensing, consumer disclosures, security, recordkeeping, authorizations, and other applicable obligations.

2. Privacy Notice

The prototype may collect account names, email addresses, phone numbers, credentials, vehicle descriptions, VINs, service requests, appointments, repair-related records, and optional customer-uploaded images.

This information supports sign-in, repair-shop account management, repair workflows, estimates, invoices, and demonstration features. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive or real-world information during testing.

The current website does not provide a working public contact-message form. Do not treat any placeholder support channel as active.

3. Terms of Use

Features may be incomplete, changed, or unavailable. The software is provided for academic and demonstration purposes and is not warranted for commercial repair management.

Do not misuse the service or enter data that you do not have permission to share.

4. California Repair Service Notice

Motivo models common automotive-service workflows, but its estimate, authorization, invoicing, and retention implementation has not been verified against every requirement applicable to a California automotive repair business.

A production deployment must independently address repair authorizations, additional-work approvals, consumer disclosures, and record retention.

5. Electronic Communications & Acknowledgment

The app may present notices, estimates, invoices, and other repair information electronically. Account acknowledgment alone does not authorize specific repair work or changes in price.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

As a prototype, test records may be retained, modified, reset, or deleted during development and maintenance. A commercial service requires an appropriate retention and deletion policy.

7. Minimum Age and Children

Motivo is a general-audience automotive-service demonstration and is not directed to children under 13. New account registration requires an affirmative statement that the registrant is at least 13 years old; date of birth is not requested.

If an account may have been created by someone under 13, the operator should review the account and associated information through an authorized administrative process.

8. Emails, Payments, and Analytics

The current project sends account and repair-workflow emails rather than operating an optional promotional mailing list. It does not have implemented automatic-renewal checkout or session-replay recording. These features would require separate review and controls before being introduced.

The application uses local/system typography; its base stylesheet is served by Motivo without fetching fonts from Google at runtime.

9. Contact

The website Contact page currently has no live messaging form or verified public contact channel. Contact your repair shop directly about a repair or appointment.
