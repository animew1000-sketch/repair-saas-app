Platform Overview

One Operating System for the Entire Repair Shop

Apex Auto SaaS connects customer intake, repair workflows, technician operations, parts tracking, estimates, and invoicing inside one multi-tenant web platform.

Core Platform Capabilities

Multiple repair businesses can operate independently using isolated company data, unique branding, and dedicated shop identities.
Managers, service advisors, technicians, billing staff, and customers receive interfaces and permissions based on their assigned roles.
Repair jobs move through customer intake, vehicle data, appointments, repair orders, parts and labor, estimates, repair execution, and invoicing.
Customers can create accounts, submit service requests, update vehicle information, track repair status, and review invoices.

Customer Intake

Capture customer, contact, and vehicle information while associating each request with a repair job.

VIN & Vehicle Tools

Store 17-digit VIN data and support vehicle lookup tools for faster service documentation.

Parts & Labor

Track parts and labor items against each repair job and support pricing workflows.

Billing & Invoicing

Generate repair totals, invoice records, and payment-status information for customers.
